Legal

Privacy

& Policy

Your trust matters as much as your smile. Here's how we protect both.

Your personal information belongs to you. We're committed to keeping it that way. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe — in plain language, not legal jargon.

What We Collect

We only gather information that helps us care for you better.

Personal Details

Full name and date of birth

Gender and preferred pronouns

Emergency contact information

Contact Information

Phone number and email address

Residential address

Preferred communication method

Health Records

Dental and medical history

Known allergies and current medications

Treatment notes and diagnostic images

How We Use Your Data

Every piece of information we collect serves a purpose.

Treatment Planning: To provide accurate diagnoses and personalized care.

Appointment Management: To schedule visits and send reminders.

Record Continuity: To maintain complete treatment history.

Service Improvement: To enhance your experience based on feedback.

Data Protection

Your information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized clinical staff.

Advanced encryption on all digital records

Restricted access to patient files

No sharing with third parties without explicit consent

Legal compliance with healthcare privacy regulations

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality and understand visitor behavior.

Essential Cookies: Required for site navigation and booking.

Analytics Cookies: Help us see which pages are most useful.

Record Continuity: To maintain complete treatment history.

Preference Cookies: Remember your settings for future visits.

You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data.

Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you

Correction: Update any inaccurate or outdated information

Deletion: Ask us to remove your data, subject to legal requirements

Portability: Transfer your records to another provider

Withdrawal: Revoke consent at any time

Policy Changes

We review this policy regularly and may update it as needed. Any changes will be posted here with the updated date.

Concerns

If anything in this policy feels unclear — or you'd like to exercise your rights — we're here to help.
