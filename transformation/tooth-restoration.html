Patient's Story

Daniel is a 47-year-old high school teacher who had spent months living with a cracked molar. Every meal became a careful negotiation. Hot coffee sent sharp pain through his jaw. Cold water was unbearable. Chewing on his right side was impossible.

He'd been told by another clinic that the tooth couldn't be saved – extraction was the only option.

He came to us for a second opinion.

Concern


  • Cracked molar from years of nighttime grinding


  • Pain when biting or chewing


  • Sensitivity to hot and cold


  • Previous clinic recommended extraction


  • Patient feared losing his natural tooth

The Solution

Using 3D CBCT imaging, we mapped the full extent of the damage. The root structure was still healthy. The bone was intact. The tooth could be saved.


  • Micro-surgical root canal


  • Custom post placement


  • Porcelain crown

Results

Today, Daniel eats without thinking twice. The sensitivity is gone. The pain is a memory. The crown blends seamlessly with his natural teeth – no one can tell which tooth was ever in trouble.


  • Tooth saved from extraction


  • Zero pain or sensitivity


  • Full chewing function on both sides


  • Natural-looking porcelain crown


  • No visible difference from natural teeth

Patient Quote

"I was convinced I was going to lose the tooth. They saved it – and you'd never know anything was ever wrong. I can eat whatever I want again. The whole experience was easier than I expected."
